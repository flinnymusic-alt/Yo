Friend groups: a feed of just your people

Right now the story row mixes close friends with news channels and public figures, and you can only filter by one account at a time. This concept adds a filter row above the feed so you can switch to all your friends, all creators, or a group you made yourself.

How accounts get sorted

  • FriendYou follow each other. Mutual follows are a strong signal that it's someone you know.
  • CreatorVerified, or a channel with a large following that doesn't follow you back.
  • OverrideAny post's menu lets you move someone between Friend and Creator, so a friend whose channel blew up stays a friend.

Try it

  1. Tap Friends. The seven creator channels disappear from the feed and the story row.
  2. Drag any picture or name (story row or a post) onto a group. Drop it on a chip at the top, or on a group in the tray that slides up from the bottom. On a phone, press and hold first. Adding someone copies them, so they stay in their other groups too.
  3. Drag someone onto + New group to start a group with them.
  4. Open Family and drag someone out onto Remove from Family. Tap Undo if you change your mind. Drop them on another group instead to copy them there.
  5. Tap ⋯ on any post to add that person to a group.
  6. Tap an avatar in the story row to see one person, as the app works today.
